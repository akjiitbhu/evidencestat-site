Our Experts

The people behind the evidence

Dr. Ram Bajpai

Biostatistics and Statistical Research

Dr Ram Bajpai is an experienced medical statistician specialising in advanced statistical methodology, quantitative epidemiology, evidence synthesis, and predictive modelling in healthcare. With an academic career spanning the UK, Singapore, and India, he combines methodological expertise with practical research insight to support researchers, clinicians, and healthcare organisations in producing robust, transparent, and impactful evidence. He has authored or co-authored more than 150 peer-reviewed publications, with over 10,000 citations and an h-index of 40. His research has appeared in journals including Nature, The Lancet, The BMJ, BMJ Medicine, eClinicalMedicine, Annals of the Rheumatic Diseases, and BMC Medical Research Methodology.

Beyond his research, Ram has held editorial and statistical editorial roles for international journals, contributed to Cochrane methodological initiatives, and served on the NIHR Pre-doctoral Fellowship assessment panel. He is a Fellow of the Royal Statistical Society and the Higher Education Academy, and a Global Faculty Affiliate at VinUniversity, Vietnam. He is known for his rigorous analytical approach, collaborative working style, and commitment to research excellence.

Medical Writing Professional

Shalu Chauhan is a medical writing professional with a Ph.D. in Pharmacology and extensive experience as a Senior Medical Writer. Her expertise spans clinical and regulatory documentation, scientific literature, and pharmacovigilance content.
